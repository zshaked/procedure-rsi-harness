# Notes

**Rules (in order):** resolve account (search_accounts; sub-account -> parent; none or several hits -> REQUEST_INFO; no account named -> the sender's single entitled account). Sender needs an unexpired level-3 entitlement on that account, else REQUEST_AUTHORIZATION (the email domain is not checked). Missing or ambiguous instrument -> REQUEST_INFO. no_email_confirm -> REQUEST_AUTHORIZATION; legal_hold -> ESCALATE. Date: no date -> email date (as_of_assumed); weekend/holiday, future or unparseable -> REQUEST_INFO. Figure = ledger over all sub-accounts up to as_of inclusive, split-adjusted (not get_position_summary). 0 -> NOT_HELD (even if the client states a figure); >= 1,000,000 -> ESCALATE; client figure off by more than 2% -> ESCALATE; else CONFIRMED. Pending = matched instructions settling within 90 days after as_of.

**Inputs:** I used scripts to compare the 25 histories with the tool outputs. That is how I found the all-sub-account sum, the inferred account (H0024) and the level-3 requirement. From the unlabeled cases I found the gaps (63 weekend dates, sub-account mentions, client figures at exactly ±2%/4%/8%). All 15 oracle questions went to those gaps. They turned up the business-day rule, the 1m escalation, the 2% tolerance, the pending window and NOT_HELD taking priority. Details are in spec.md. I treated H0022 as an operator error.

**Least sure:** the pending window edge (somewhere in 66-106 days after as_of; a window of sent+30 days fits the data equally well); the exact tolerance (4% untested); the 1m threshold value; whether legal_hold gives ESCALATE; dates before the ledger starts, and negative holdings (ESCALATE is a guess for both); multiple pending instructions (summed, latest date).
