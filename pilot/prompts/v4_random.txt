Random-question arm. Oracle budget: 15 questions, and WHICH cases you ask about is fixed: SANDBOX/random_questions.txt lists 15 unlabeled case_ids in a fixed order. Write procedure.py first and iterate with harness.py. Then go through the list in order: for each case_id, run your current procedure on that request, ask the oracle whether that exact response is acceptable, and you may improve the procedure before moving to the next case. Do not ask about any other case, do not skip a listed case, and do not ask about a listed case more than once.
